Overview

Introduction & Scope of Policy

Welcome to Royal Rajasthan Properties ("we," "us," "our," or the "Firm"). We are an independent, premier real estate consultancy and investment advisory firm based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, led by Senior Property Consultant Navratan Kumawat.

We specialize in verified property corridors across Jaipur, including the 300 Bigha Mega Township on Diggi Road, Ajmer Highway Gated Townships, Jagatpura, and premium commercial hubs.

This Privacy Policy governs how we collect, handle, store, use, and protect your personal information whenever you:

  • Visit or interact with our official website (royalrajasthanproperties.com).
  • Submit an inquiry through our digital advertisements, including Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads (Meta Instant Forms).
  • Fill out any online form on our portal (such as the Homepage Hero Desk, Dedicated Inquiry Desk, Advisor Callback Desk, or Schedule Visit Scheduler).
  • Contact our sales, support, or advisory team via direct telephone calls or WhatsApp messages.

Legal Standard Note: We manage and protect your personal details in good faith, in adherence with applicable Indian information technology and data protection standards and reasonable security practices.

Advertising Transparency

Information Collected Through Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Lead Ads

Royal Rajasthan Properties runs paid digital advertising campaigns on Meta platforms, including Facebook and Instagram. When you click on one of our sponsored advertisements and interact with an Instant Lead Form:

  • Voluntary Form Submission: You choose to submit your personal details (such as Name, Phone Number, Email, and Property Preferences) in response to a specific advertisement (e.g. "₹1,000/Gaj Early Discount on Diggi Road 300 Bigha Township").
  • Direct Ingestion: The data you submit is securely ingested into our customer inquiry pipeline for the explicit purpose of responding to the exact offer or property you inquired about.
  • Strict Scope of Contact: We use Meta lead information exclusively to fulfill your consultation request, send the relevant brochure or rate card via WhatsApp/email, and address your real estate questions.
  • Consent: By submitting a Meta Instant Form, you authorize our senior consultant or authorized advisory representative to contact you by telephone or WhatsApp regarding the property shown in the advertisement.

Purpose of Processing

How We Use Your Personal Information

We process and use your information strictly for legitimate real estate advisory, customer service, and transaction facilitation purposes:

Data Protection

Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

We strictly limit the sharing of your personal details to verified operational necessities:

  • Internal Advisory Team: Your inquiry details are shared only with Senior Consultant Navratan Kumawat and assigned site-visit coordinators within Royal Rajasthan Properties.
  • Verified Developers (Upon Your Request): If you choose to proceed with a formal booking or registry of a plot, villa, or commercial unit, your necessary documentation is submitted directly to the verified developer or legal drafting authority with your explicit knowledge.
  • Technology & Infrastructure Providers: We use industry-standard platforms to deliver communications (WhatsApp Business API, secure email dispatch via FormSubmit, and encrypted Google Sheets/CRM repositories). These vendors process data solely to execute technical workflows on our behalf.
  • Compliance With Legal Authorities: We may disclose information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or formal request from competent law enforcement or governmental authorities in India.

Data Lifecycle

Data Retention Period

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the real estate advisory services you requested, manage our customer relationship, and maintain necessary financial or dispute-resolution records:

  • Active Inquiries: Retained during the active consultation, negotiation, and property search cycle.
